# 🛒 E-Commerce Data Analytics Project

### End-to-End Business Intelligence & Customer Analytics

**Project Type:** Data Analytics | Business Intelligence | Customer Segmentation

**Tools & Technologies:** Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI, Tableau, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing an E-Commerce business using real-world transactional data from the Brazilian Olist E-Commerce dataset.

The objective is to extract meaningful business insights, understand customer purchasing behavior, analyze revenue trends, and improve customer retention through data-driven recommendations.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall E-Commerce sales performance.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Analyze customer purchasing patterns.
- Perform RFM Customer Segmentation.
- Evaluate customer retention using Cohort Analysis.
- Analyze payment methods and order trends.
- Develop interactive business dashboards.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.
